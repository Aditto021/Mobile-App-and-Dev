A shopping app for Bangladesh: phones, gadgets, fashion and beauty in one place, with prices in Taka, a synced cart and wishlist, vouchers and live order tracking. Made by Group G-10 for CSE 464 at Independent University, Bangladesh.

Checkout is a demonstration: no money is charged and no goods are shipped.
