Delete your feriWala account

feriWala for Android, by Group G-10 (IUB)

In the app (instant)

  1. Open feriWala and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Without the app

Email support@feriwala.com from the email address (or tell us the mobile number) you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my feriWala account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your name, email or mobile number, password hash, delivery address, cart, voucher, wishlist and order history — all of it, from our server. Nothing is kept afterwards.
