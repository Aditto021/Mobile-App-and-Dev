Privacy policy

feriWala for Android · Updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

feriWala is a shopping app made by Group G-10 for the CSE 464 course at Independent University, Bangladesh. Contact us at support@feriwala.com.

What we collect

If you create an account: your name, your email address or mobile number, your password (kept only as a salted hash, never as text) and your delivery address.

While you shop: your cart, voucher, wishlist and orders (items, prices, payment method name and delivery address).

As a guest, all of this stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

What we do not collect

Card numbers, CVVs and bKash or Nagad numbers are only checked on your phone and are never sent or stored — checkout is a demonstration and no money is charged. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

How we use it

Only to run your account: sign you in, keep your cart and wishlist the same on every device, and record your orders. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Others who see some data

Product photos are loaded from the image servers they are published on. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address when a photo loads.

Security

The app talks to our server over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a random salt, and sign-ins expire after 30 days.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account until you delete it. Account → Delete account in the app removes your account, cart, wishlist and order history from our server at once, and from your phone. You can also ask us to delete it.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app with a new date.
